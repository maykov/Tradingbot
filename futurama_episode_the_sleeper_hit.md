# Futurama — New Episode Concept

## Title: "The Sleeper Hit"

**Logline:** When Professor Farnsworth invents a device that can extract and stream sleep, Mom turns it into "DreamStream™" — a subscription service where the exhausted rich buy sleep from the desperate poor. Fry, whose brain lacks the delta wave, turns out to be the only person whose sleep can be streamed without side effects, and becomes the involuntary sleeping heart of New New York — until one of his nightmares goes out live to eight million subscribers.

**Themes:** The gig economy, subscription-everything culture, sleep deprivation as a status symbol, and Fry & Leela — how being "always on" costs you the people you love.

**Continuity hook:** Fry's missing delta brainwave (established in "The Day the Earth Stood Stupid") is the reason his sleep is uniquely "clean" and streamable.

## Scene Descriptions

### Scene 1 — Cold Open: The All-Nighter
The Planet Express ship limps home at dawn after a 72-hour non-stop delivery of alarm clocks to the Insomniac Nebula. Everyone is wrecked — Leela is flying with her eye taped open, the Professor has fallen asleep standing up, Zoidberg is sleep-scuttling in circles. Only Fry is fresh as a daisy: he slept through the entire job, including a meteor storm, a boarding by space pirates, and the in-flight movie. Gag runner: Fry can sleep through *anything*.

### Scene 2 — Good News, Everyone: The Somno-Siphon
The Professor unveils the **Somno-Siphon**, a colander-and-jumper-cables headset that extracts sleep from one brain and injects it into another, "finally decoupling sleep from the lazy act of sleeping." Demo on Zoidberg goes horribly (Hermes receives Zoidberg's dream and briefly believes he is a crab). Key exposition: dreams ride along with the sleep unless the donor's brainwaves are "pure."

### Scene 3 — Mom Smells Money
Mom, tipped off by her sons' surveillance of the Professor, sweet-talks/strong-arms the patent away and launches **DreamStream™** — "Sleep is a subscription now, dearies." Mass screening of donors fails: everyone's dreams contaminate the stream. Then the scanner hits Fry — no delta wave, a perfectly silent, dreamless-on-demand brain. Mom's eyes turn to dollar signs. Fry signs a contract he doesn't read ("It said free snacks!").

### Scene 4 — DreamStream Nation (montage)
New New York transforms. Billboards: "WHY SLEEP WHEN THE SLEEPER SLEEPS FOR YOU?" The rich work, party, and stay awake 24/7 while renting Fry's sleep; the poor sell their waking hours covering night shifts. Fry is a celebrity — "The Sleeper" merch, talk shows he attends asleep. Hypnotoad ratings collapse. Leela watches Fry drift away from her, one nap at a time.

### Scene 5 — The Golden Pod
MomCorp's Sleep Ranch: Fry lives in a diamond-encrusted sleep pod, awake only 20 minutes a day, groggy and rich. Leela visits during his waking window; the conversation is cut short when the "SLEEP QUOTA" klaxon sounds and technicians re-sedate him mid-sentence — right as he's trying to tell Leela something important. Leela's hand on the pod glass. Emotional low point.

### Scene 6 — The Nightmare Broadcast
Overworked and over-sedated, Fry's brain finally produces something it never has: a nightmare — losing Leela in a collapsing Old New York while a giant Slurm worm wearing Mom's face devours the city. It streams live to 8 million subscribers. Citizens drop mid-street into shared night terrors. Chaos: a screaming Chief O'Mannahan, URL the cop sobbing about a worm, the Mayor declares a State of Nightmare.

### Scene 7 — Operation Wake-Up Call
At Planet Express, the news plays as the crew regroups. The Professor explains Fry can't be woken from outside — he's in a sedation feedback loop; someone must enter the stream and convince him to *want* to wake up. Leela volunteers instantly. Bender, who has been selling counterfeit bootleg dreams ("100% pure, stepped-on only twice"), gets dragged along because his bootleg rig can hack a DreamStream node. Break-in at MomCorp: Amy and Hermes run distraction; Zoidberg is the distraction.

### Scene 8 — Inside the Dream
Leela dives into Fry's dreamscape: a golden, slightly-wrong Old New York, ca. 2000 — Panucci's Pizza, his old bike, dream-versions of his family with TV-static faces. She finds Fry happy and half-dissolved into the dream, forgetting the waking world. Nightmare-Mom (the Slurm-worm colossus) attacks; Leela fights, but the dream only breaks when Fry realizes what he'd been trying to tell Leela in Scene 5 — and that it's worthless if he only ever says it in a dream. He chooses to wake. The dream collapses upward into light.

### Scene 9 — Crash & Tag
Meanwhile in the server room, Bender — trying to steal premium dream files — overloads the DreamStream mainframe with his own backed-up dreams (all of them about killing all humans, none of them streamable). Servers explode; the city jolts awake; Mom's empire of naps collapses in a class-action avalanche. Fry wakes with Leela beside the pod; he finally says it awake: nothing fancy, just "I'd rather be tired with you." Tag scene: the Professor salvages one server; Zoidberg's dreams accidentally broadcast to the break room; everyone screams. Iris out.
