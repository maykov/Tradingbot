### SHOT 2.1 — 12s

- **Shot type:** Extreme wide
- **Angle & height:** Eye level, camera positioned as if looking in through the window glass — a quietly voyeuristic vantage, as though studying a specimen in a jar
- **Lens & DoF:** 24mm, deep focus (table props sharp foreground to back wall)
- **Camera movement:** Static, locked-off
- **Framing & composition:** Full room in frame: door wall with the sealed main entrance and small hinged window panel screen right; the upright piano (open, Chopin sheet music on the stand) on the east wall; the iron stove and narrow made bed on the north wall; the long oak table (stack of twelve yellow-backed novels, inkstand, water carafe) center; the west casement window camera left, daylight raking across the floorboards. MOROZOV's black frock coat hangs brushed on its nail beside the door.
- **Blocking:** NIKOLAI ANDREYEVICH MOROZOV, 26, open-collared shirt, unbuttoned black waistcoat, felt house shoes, paces the room's diagonal — eight steps to the stove corner, turn, eight steps back to the door corner — passing the bed and glancing once, without stopping, at the window panel.
- **Dialogue & performance:** None (no dialogue)
- **Lighting:** Soft daylight (5600K) through the west casement crossing the table and floor; deep-orange stove-door glow as fill from the north wall; his long shadow sweeps the boards with each pass.
- **Color grade & mood:** Desaturated cool grays and chalk whites with one warm honey highlight at the table and stove — monastic, contained, quietly tense.
- **Duration:** 12 seconds
- **Transition out:** Cut
