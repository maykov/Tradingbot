## SCENE 3 — YEAR FIVE

**Location:** INT. GARDEN LODGE — MAIN ROOM | **Time:** NIGHT (1890) | **Total duration: 150 seconds**

---

### SHOT 3.1 — [10]s

- **Shot type:** Extreme wide
- **Angle & height:** Eye level, slightly elevated (chest-height on a short riser), surveying the whole room
- **Lens & DoF:** 21mm, deep focus (f/8) — everything from the piano to the bed reads sharp
- **Camera movement:** Static, held
- **Framing & composition:** Foreground: the shut, dust-filmed PIANO lid and the rank of 34 empty dark-green wine bottles along the east wall. Midground: the littered oak table — dirty plates, bread crusts, the overturned inkwell's dried black stain in the grain. Background: NIKOLAI ANDREYEVICH MOROZOV, small and still, on the narrow iron bed against the north wall, half-lit by the kerosene lamp's pool of light on the table between camera and bed.
- **Blocking:** MOROZOV lies flat on the unmade bed, gray sheets twisted beneath him, one arm across his chest. No movement yet.
- **Dialogue & performance:** None (no dialogue)
- **Lighting:** Single brass kerosene lamp on the table, 2000K, casting a warm 1-meter pool that fades to near-black at the walls; a low deep-orange stove-glow bleeds faintly from the cold stove's door seam on the north wall; everything beyond both sources falls to crushed shadow.
- **Color grade & mood:** Desaturated bottle-green and rust-brown palette, blacks crushed, minimal warm highlight only around the lamp; the room reads as an entombed monochrome cell. Mood: rot, stasis, decay made visible.
- **Duration:** 10 seconds
- **Transition out:** Cut

---

### SHOT 3.2 — [8]s

- **Shot type:** Medium
- **Angle & height:** Eye level, camera at bed height
- **Lens & DoF:** 50mm, shallow-medium (f/2.8) — Morozov sharp, bottles and stove soft in background
- **Camera movement:** Slow dolly-in, imperceptible, 8 seconds to close roughly one meter
- **Framing & composition:** MOROZOV fills the center frame on the bed, wearing his wine-stained bottle-green dressing gown open over a gray undershirt, barefoot, one bare foot hanging off the mattress edge. A full glass of dark red wine stands on the chair beside the bed, foreground right, slightly out of focus. Felt shoes lie abandoned mid-floor, background left.
- **Blocking:** MOROZOV stares straight up at the ceiling beam, unmoving except for shallow breathing. His long, unwashed shoulder-length hair fans on the pillow; four-month patchy beard catches lamp edge-light.
- **Dialogue & performance:** None (no dialogue)
- **Lighting:** Same kerosene lamp key from off-frame left (table), low-angle, throwing a hard shadow of the chair-back across his legs; stove-glow fill from the north wall barely warms his profile; face otherwise underlit, eye sockets shadowed.
- **Color grade & mood:** Sallow ivory skin against faded bottle-green wool; wine-dark red the only saturated note in frame. Mood: torpor, a man marinating in his own ruin.
- **Duration:** 8 seconds
- **Transition out:** Cut

### SHOT 3.3 — [12]s

- **Shot type:** Close-up
- **Angle & height:** Eye level, camera just above his face looking slightly down, as if standing over the bed
- **Lens & DoF:** 85mm, very shallow (f/1.8) — only the eyes and mouth in critical focus, pillow and hair falling soft
- **Camera movement:** Static, held on the face
- **Framing & composition:** Morozov's puffy, bloodstained-lipped face fills the frame, bloodshot eyes fixed on the ceiling beam above, unblinking for long stretches.
- **Blocking:** Only his lips move; the rest of the body stays rigid on the bed. No eye contact with camera — his eyeline holds on the beam overhead throughout.
- **Dialogue & performance:** **MOROZOV** (muttering, to the ceiling, half-voiced, the words slurred and swallowed at the edges, rhythm obsessive like a man replaying an old wound): *"...and I said to him — I said — fifteen years, to the day... to the day..."* Delivered slow, bitter, almost tender on "to the day," the second repetition trailing into silence, eyes glassing over.
- **Lighting:** Practical kerosene lamp key from low camera-left, hard and unflattering, carving deep shadow under the cheekbones and brow; no fill — the far side of his face dissolves into black.
- **Color grade & mood:** Warm amber key against crushed black shadow; skin pushed toward sallow yellow-green. Mood: a man arguing with a ghost of himself, fifteen years compressed into two repeated words.
- **Duration:** 12 seconds
- **Transition out:** Cut

### SHOT 3.4 — [10]s

- **Shot type:** Medium
- **Angle & height:** Eye level, slightly low, looking up along the length of the bed
- **Lens & DoF:** 40mm, medium depth (f/4) — bed, chair, and bottle-rank all legible
- **Camera movement:** Static, held
- **Framing & composition:** Morozov still supine, foreground his torso and the dressing gown's stained lapel; midground the wine glass on the chair; background the dark rank of empty bottles along the east wall, their green glass catching a faint lamp glint.
- **Blocking:** Without sitting up, Morozov reaches sideways, closes his hand around the glass on the chair, lifts his head only enough to drink. He drains it in one continuous motion, throat working, then drops his head back to the pillow.
- **Dialogue & performance:** He laughs — a short, ugly, humorless bark, cut off almost as soon as it starts. No words.
- **Lighting:** Same lamp key, now catching the wine's surface as a small red glint mid-shot; stove-glow fill unchanged on the north side of frame.
- **Color grade & mood:** The wine-red glint is the shot's single saturated accent against the desaturated green-black room. Mood: contempt turned inward, a joke with no one to hear it.
- **Duration:** 10 seconds
- **Transition out:** Cut

### SHOT 3.5 — [8]s

- **Shot type:** Insert
- **Angle & height:** High angle, near-overhead, looking straight down at floor level beside the bed
- **Lens & DoF:** 60mm macro, shallow (f/2) — the bottle and glass rim sharp, floorboards falling soft
- **Camera movement:** Static, held
- **Framing & composition:** Tight on an open wine bottle standing on the floorboards, Morozov's bare arm and long-nailed hand entering frame from top, tilting the bottle to refill the glass without lifting it from where it rests near the bed's edge.
- **Blocking:** Morozov's arm hangs off the mattress, wrist limp until it isn't — the pour is practiced, unthinking, a motion his body performs without his mind's participation. He never sits up.
- **Dialogue & performance:** None (no dialogue) — only the faint glug of the pour
- **Lighting:** Hard spill from the table lamp raking low across the floor, catching the bottle's dark green glass as a rim-light; the rest of the insert falls into near-black.
- **Color grade & mood:** A single hard highlight on wet glass in an otherwise black frame. Mood: mechanical, addicted, joyless repetition.
- **Duration:** 8 seconds
- **Transition out:** Cut

### SHOT 3.6 — [12]s

- **Shot type:** Wide
- **Angle & height:** Eye level, camera positioned at the foot of the table, facing Morozov across it
- **Lens & DoF:** 35mm, medium-deep (f/5.6) — table, lamp, and Morozov all in focus; bed and piano soft in the deep background
- **Camera movement:** Slow dissolve-in from Shot 3.5 marking the time jump to LATER in the night; once resolved, camera is static
- **Framing & composition:** MOROZOV now seated at the long oak table, foreground the kerosene lamp burning low, its light pooling on a spreading stack of handwritten pages; midground his hunched shoulders and moving pen hand; background the shut piano and the bottle-rank, both receding into shadow.
- **Blocking:** He writes furiously, page after page, barely pausing between sheets, tossing each finished page onto a growing pile at his elbow. His unbelted robe has slipped further open; hair falls forward, hiding his face.
- **Dialogue & performance:** None (no dialogue) — only the scratch of the nib, fast and hard, occasionally tearing the paper
- **Lighting:** Kerosene lamp is now the sole and dominant key, close and warm on his hands and the page, falling off sharply beyond the table's edge into total black; stove is cold, no fill from the north wall in this later hour.
- **Color grade & mood:** Tight warm pool of amber against surrounding black, almost chiaroscuro. Mood: manic, compulsive productivity that reads, even here, as another form of despair.
- **Duration:** 12 seconds
- **Transition out:** Cut

### SHOT 3.7 — [8]s

- **Shot type:** Close-up / insert
- **Angle & height:** High angle, looking down at the tabletop
- **Lens & DoF:** 90mm macro, very shallow (f/2) — pen nib and top page razor-sharp, rest of the stack falling soft
- **Camera movement:** Slow pan, left to right, following the pen's line across the page
- **Framing & composition:** His ink-darkened fingers and the steel-nibbed pen dominate the frame; beneath the hand, dense cramped handwriting fills page after page of the manuscript, the pile now visibly twenty-plus sheets deep.
- **Blocking:** The hand does not stop moving; long fingernails scrape faintly against the paper with each stroke.
- **Dialogue & performance:** None (no dialogue)
- **Lighting:** Lamp key directly overhead-left, hard specular glint off the wet ink; no fill, edges of frame vignette to black.
- **Color grade & mood:** High-contrast amber-on-black, ink glinting near-black-blue. Mood: obsession, a mind trying to outrun itself on paper.
- **Duration:** 8 seconds
- **Transition out:** Cut

### SHOT 3.8 — [10]s

- **Shot type:** Medium
- **Angle & height:** Eye level, camera square to his shoulder, three-quarter profile
- **Lens & DoF:** 50mm, shallow (f/2.8) — Morozov sharp, table clutter soft
- **Camera movement:** Static, held
- **Framing & composition:** Morozov leans back from the table, the finished manuscript stack now filling the frame's lower third; he lifts the topmost pages into the lamplight to reread them.
- **Blocking:** He stops writing abruptly, sets down the pen, gathers the top sheets, and begins to read back through what he has written, lips moving faintly at first, then falling still.
- **Dialogue & performance:** None (no dialogue) — his breathing audibly slows, then catches
- **Lighting:** Lamp key unchanged, now lighting the raised page and the underside of his jaw, throwing his eye sockets into deep shadow as he reads downward.
- **Color grade & mood:** Same tight amber pool, but the mood shifts — anticipation curdling. Mood: a man about to recognize his own failure.
- **Duration:** 10 seconds
- **Transition out:** Cut

### SHOT 3.9 — [8]s

- **Shot type:** Close-up
- **Angle & height:** Eye level, dead-on
- **Lens & DoF:** 85mm, shallow (f/2) — face isolated, page in hand blurred at the frame's lower edge
- **Camera movement:** Static, held
- **Framing & composition:** Morozov's face fills the frame, the manuscript page held just below it, out of focus.
- **Blocking:** His eyes move rapidly line to line, then stop. His jaw tightens. The face goes still — not angry, worse: disgusted.
- **Dialogue & performance:** None (no dialogue) — pure facial performance: a controlled contempt settling over the features, nostrils flaring once, then nothing
- **Lighting:** Lamp key low from below-frame, uplighting in a way that hollows the cheeks and turns the disgust almost skull-like.
- **Color grade & mood:** Sickly amber-green skin tones, heavy shadow. Mood: self-loathing crystallizing into decision.
- **Duration:** 8 seconds
- **Transition out:** Cut

### SHOT 3.10 — [12]s

- **Shot type:** Medium
- **Angle & height:** Eye level, slightly high, looking down at the table's surface and his hands
- **Lens & DoF:** 40mm, medium (f/4) — hands and pages sharp, background soft
- **Camera movement:** Handheld, subtle sway, tightening almost imperceptibly as the tearing accelerates
- **Framing & composition:** Morozov gathers the entire manuscript — twenty, thirty pages — into both hands, foreground; midground the lamp; background the dark room.
- **Blocking:** He rises half out of the chair, squares the stack against the table, and begins to tear it — not in a single furious rip but methodically, sheet by sheet, then the sheets again into quarters, dropping the pieces where they fall.
- **Dialogue & performance:** None (no dialogue) — only the dry, repeated sound of tearing paper, steady as a metronome
- **Lighting:** Lamp key catches the falling scraps as they cross its pool of light, flickering bright-dark-bright as pieces fall through the beam.
- **Color grade & mood:** Warm light fragmenting into a blizzard of pale scraps against black. Mood: ritualized self-destruction, calm on the surface, annihilating underneath.
- **Duration:** 12 seconds
- **Transition out:** Cut

### SHOT 3.11 — [8]s

- **Shot type:** Close-up
- **Angle & height:** Eye level, close on the hands and table surface
- **Lens & DoF:** 70mm, shallow (f/2.5) — hands and torn edges sharp, everything past them dissolving to soft black
- **Camera movement:** Static, held

- **Blocking:** The tearing continues without pause, mechanical now, his breathing the only other sound.
- **Dialogue & performance:** None (no dialogue)
- **Lighting:** Same lamp key, hard top-light on the paper making the scraps read almost luminous white against the dark wood and dark hands.
- **Color grade & mood:** Stark white-on-black graphic quality. Mood: the visual crescendo of the destruction.
- **Duration:** 8 seconds

### SHOT 3.12 — [12]s

- **Shot type:** Wide
- **Angle & height:** Eye level, camera pulled back to take in the whole table and the floor around it
- **Lens & DoF:** 28mm, deep focus (f/5.6) — the entire wreckage legible from lamp to floorboards
- **Camera movement:** Static, held, no movement for the full duration — deliberately inert after the frantic tearing
- **Framing & composition:** Morozov sits motionless in the chair, foreground center, surrounded by torn paper scraps covering the table and the floor around him like fallen snow; background the shut dusty piano and the bottle-rank, both dimly visible at the edge of the lamp's reach.
- **Blocking:** He does not move. Hands empty now, resting on the table's edge among the scraps. His head is bowed but not yet down.
- **Dialogue & performance:** None (no dialogue) — total stillness, the room's silence suddenly enormous after the tearing
- **Lighting:** Lamp key unchanged, now reading as a small, lonely island of light in a large dark room; the scraps catch and scatter it faintly across the floor.
- **Color grade & mood:** Cold surrounding black pressing in on the one warm pool. Mood: exhaustion, the aftermath of a small private catastrophe.
- **Duration:** 12 seconds
- **Transition out:** Cut

### SHOT 3.13 — [10]s

- **Shot type:** Medium / close-up (push-in)
- **Angle & height:** Eye level, slightly side-on to catch his face as it lowers
- **Lens & DoF:** 55mm, shallow (f/2.2) — his head and crossed arms sharp, table scraps softening in foreground
- **Camera movement:** Slow, quiet push-in, dolly forward roughly half a meter over the full duration
- **Framing & composition:** Morozov's crossed arms and bowed head fill the center of frame, cushioned on the litter of torn manuscript pages; the lamp's light falls directly across the back of his neck and hair.
- **Blocking:** He lowers his head onto his forearms among the scraps. His shoulders begin to shake — small at first, then in full, silent heaves.
- **Dialogue & performance:** None (no dialogue) — he WEEPS, the full, hopeless weeping of a man convinced no one can hear him; breath audible, ragged, swallowed sobs rather than cries
- **Lighting:** Lamp key raking across his hair and the pale scraps, leaving his hidden face in near-total shadow — we read the weeping entirely through his shoulders and breath, not his features.
- **Color grade & mood:** Warm light on a huddled, defeated shape surrounded by white paper wreckage in a black room. Mood: the scene's emotional floor — total private despair.
- **Duration:** 10 seconds
- **Transition out:** Cut

### SHOT 3.15 — [14]s

- **Shot type:** Close-up
- **Angle & height:** Eye level, reverse angle from outside the lodge, on Stepan's face and hands at the panel
- **Lens & DoF:** 65mm, shallow (f/2.5) — Stepan's face and hand sharp, the lodge wall behind falling soft
- **Camera movement:** Static, held for the first half; almost imperceptible slow tilt-down following his hand as it closes the panel
- **Framing & composition:** STEPAN's weathered, white-bearded face fills the left of frame, milky-blue eyes still fixed inward for a moment; his enormous knotted hand occupies the right, resting on the panel's edge. Behind him, only blackness — the night garden.
- **Blocking:** Stepan draws back from the gap, crosses himself slowly with his free hand — forehead, chest, right shoulder, left shoulder — then, without a sound, closes the panel until it clicks shut, sealing the warm light away entirely. He lingers a beat in the resulting darkness, then turns and is gone.
- **Dialogue & performance:** None (no dialogue) — his lips move faintly in a private, wordless prayer as he crosses himself; otherwise total silence except the panel's soft wooden clack
- **Lighting:** The last warm sliver of interior lamplight narrows across his face and hand as the panel swings shut, then vanishes, leaving him in cold near-black; a last, loud page-turn SOUND (unseen, from inside) punctuates the cut to black.
- **Color grade & mood:** The shot drains from a thin warm blade of light to total cold blue-black by its end. Mood: quiet reverence and pity, the audience's last human contact before the scene is sealed away — immediately undercut by the harsh, jarring sound that drives the cut.
- **Duration:** 14 seconds
- **Transition out:** Hard cut (on the loud sound of a page turning — crisp, sudden — into bright daylight, Scene 4)
