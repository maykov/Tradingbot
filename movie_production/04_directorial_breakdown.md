### SHOT 1.1 — [16s]

- **Shot type:** Wide (establishing master)
- **Angle & height:** Eye level, camera positioned near the double entrance doors on the south wall, looking north down the room's long axis
- **Lens & DoF:** 24mm, deep focus (f/8) — entire room legible from card tables to fireplace
- **Camera movement:** Slow dolly-in, 1.5 meters over the shot, coming to rest just past the potted palms
- **Framing & composition:** Foreground: brass jardinière with a 2m potted palm and the edge of a green-baize card table with abandoned cards and ivory counters. Midground: the crimson Persian carpet (indigo border), the crimson-velvet sofa and two cream-and-crimson bergère chairs grouped before the fire. Background: PYOTR SEMYONOVICH VOLKOV at the white Carrara-marble fireplace beneath his own 1.5m portrait, cigar in hand; NIKOLAI ANDREYEVICH MOROZOV forward on the edge of the east bergère, wine glass untouched; eight GUESTS scattered in evening dress, ORLOV among them near the brandy table; the two-tier crystal chandelier with 24 lit candles glowing overhead; the three tall French windows on the west wall read as cold blue-black rectangles.
- **Blocking:** Volkov stands squarely at the fireplace, weight on both feet, master-of-the-room posture. Morozov sits forward, spine straight, listening with visible disagreement, hands on his knees. Orlov stands mid-room with a brandy balloon, near the low mahogany table where his red notebook and silver pencil rest.
- **Dialogue & performance:** None (no dialogue) — pure establishing shot, ambient party murmur under
- **Lighting:** Chandelier (24 candles, ~1900K) as primary overhead source; fireplace flame as warm practical fill from camera-left background; silver candelabrum (5 candles) on the west console as secondary fill; two etched-glass oil lamps on side tables as low soft fill. Deep viridian-green damask wallpaper reads dark and rich in the shadow falloff; gilt cornices catch specular highlights. Cigar smoke visibly hazes the air, backlit by the chandelier.
- **Color grade & mood:** Warm amber-gold over deep green — opulent, convivial, unhurried. Blacks lifted slightly to keep the wallpaper detail; highlights bloom softly around the candle flames.
- **Duration:** 16 seconds
- **Transition out:** Cut
